## 2. Shop Observation

### Order Management

The purpose of the visit was to understand how Regent Tailor manages customer orders and maintains information about garments, measurements, and delivery commitments.

The observation focused on understanding the existing order-recording process and identifying whether the owner relies on manual records or digital tools to organize customer requirements.

### Deadline Management

An important area of investigation was how the owner keeps track of promised delivery dates and manages orders with different deadlines.

StitchFlow was initially conceived around the possibility that managing multiple delivery commitments could become difficult without a centralized overview.

### Work Prioritization

The investigation also focused on understanding how the owner decides which orders should be completed first, particularly when multiple customers have different delivery requirements.
